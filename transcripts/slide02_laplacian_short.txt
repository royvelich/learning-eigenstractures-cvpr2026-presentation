Narration
---------

[click 0]   A cloud of data points — a data manifold.

[click 1]   Assign a scalar function to it — one value per point.

[click 2]   Pick any point.

[click 3]   The Laplacian measures how its value deviates from the
            average of its neighbours.

[click 4]   Do that everywhere — that's the Laplacian of the function.
