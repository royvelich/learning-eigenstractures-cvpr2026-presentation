Narration
---------

[click 0 — grey point cloud is on screen]

  Imagine we're given a cloud of data points — a data manifold.

[click 1 — colour appears across the manifold]

  Now suppose we have a scalar function defined on it,
  assigning one value to every point.

[click 2 — one point on the manifold lights up]

  Pick any point.

[click 3 — the phrase "deviation from local average" fades in]

  The Laplacian at that point measures how much its value
  deviates from the average of its local neighbours.

[click 4 — the Laplacian heatmap appears across the manifold]

  Do that everywhere, and we get the Laplacian of the function —
  a new scalar field that highlights where the signal varies.
